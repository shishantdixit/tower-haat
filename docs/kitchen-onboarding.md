# TowerHaat par apna kitchen kaise shuru karein

*Kitchen owners ke liye chhota guide. Har owner ke saath 15 minute baith kar karwa dein.*

## 1. App install karein
- **Android:** Chrome mein `towerhaat.com` kholein → "Install" dabayein.
- **iPhone:** Safari mein `towerhaat.com` kholein → Share button ⎙ → **Add to Home Screen**.
  iPhone par order ki notification sirf home screen wale app se aati hai.

## 2. Sign up
Apna mobile number daalein, SMS wala code daalein, naam aur tower-flat chunein.

## 3. Business register karein
**My business → Start selling**
- Kya bechte hain: **Food** (ya Products)
- Category, kitchen ka naam, thoda description ("Ghar ka khana, thali, paratha…")
- **UPI ID** (jaise `naam@okaxis`). Paisa seedha aapke UPI mein aata hai, TowerHaat beech mein nahi hai.
- FSSAI number ho to daalein.

Approval ke baad business sabko dikhne lagta hai.

## 4. Photos aur menu
- **Photos:** kitchen aur khane ki 3–4 achhi photos. Pehli photo cover banti hai.
- **Menu:** har item ka naam, price, veg/non-veg, photo. Agar din mein limited banate hain to
  **Daily limit** daalein (jaise 20 thali). Limit poori hone par item "Sold out" dikhega.
- Kisi din koi item nahi bana? Menu mein uska **Today** tick hata dein.

## 5. Timings
**Hours and charges:** kab khulta hai, kab band, aakhri order kab tak, minimum order, delivery charge.
Chhutti ho to **My business → Close now** dabayein.

## 6. Notification on karein (zaroori!)
**My business → Turn on notifications.** Naya order aate hi phone par notification aayegi.

## 7. Order aane par
1. Notification ya **Orders → New** mein order kholein.
2. **30 minute ke andar Accept karein**, warna order apne aap cancel ho jaata hai (10 minute par reminder aata hai).
3. Phir **Start preparing → Out for delivery → Mark delivered**. Customer ko har step ki khabar jaati hai.
4. Nahi ho sakta? Reason likh kar **Reject** karein.
5. **Prep summary** mein aaj ke saare orders ka item-wise total milta hai.

## 8. Payment
- **Cash:** deliver karte waqt paisa lein → **Cash collected**.
- **UPI:** customer accept ke baad seedha aapke UPI mein bhejta hai aur "I've paid" dabata hai.
  Apne UPI app mein check karein → **UPI received**. Paisa nahi aaya? **Not received** dabayein.
